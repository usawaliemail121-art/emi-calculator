About EMICalculator

Our Purpose

EMICalculator exists to make loan planning simple, transparent and free for everyone in India. Before signing a home, car or personal loan, you deserve to know exactly what your monthly commitment will be, how much of it is interest, and how your balance will reduce year by year. We put that power in your browser — no sign-ups, no paywalls, no data collection.

Monthly EMI, total interest and total payment, recalculated the moment you change any input.

How the Calculator Works

The calculator applies the standard reducing-balance EMI formula used by Indian banks and NBFCs: EMI = P × r × (1+r)n ÷ [(1+r)n − 1]. The amortization schedule is built by simulating each monthly payment, splitting it between interest on the outstanding balance and principal repayment. It is written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no external libraries, so it loads fast even on slow connections and works on any modern device.
